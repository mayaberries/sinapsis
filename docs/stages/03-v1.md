# 🚀 First version: complete online workshops

← [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) · Previous: [Validation](02-validation.md) · Next: [Pilot](04-pilot.md) →

A practitioner can run their online workshops end to end with their real participants (reminders, sessions, material, privacy, questions between sessions and payments) without using personal WhatsApp, Zoom or Google Drive.

📍 Milestone: to be created.

## 🎬 What the stage demonstrates

- A multi-session workshop with its participant list, each participant with a profile (usual location, emergency contact and billing data).
- WhatsApp reminders (templates, no conversations) and email reminders before each session.
- Turn-taking and a private space to talk with a single participant without interrupting the group.
- A shared workshop space: material before the session; material, whiteboard and worksheets during it; summary, homework and next date after it.
- A headphones reminder, a discreet "I'm not alone" signal and a switch to chat.
- Automatic reconnection to the same session.
- Participants' questions arrive in a channel with the practitioner's office hours, response time and auto-reply.
- Each participant uploads their payment receipt and CFDI data once, and the practitioner exports them.

## ✅ Exit criteria

- All test IDs assigned to this stage are green
- The pilot's first practitioner runs a full real workshop in Sinapsis
- The participant interface works on low-end Android with a limited connection
